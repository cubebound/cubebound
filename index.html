Cubebound.

A free, fan-made tool for building, sharing, and drafting Riftbound cubes.

Early development

Cube is a beloved format in trading card games: players curate a custom pool of cards, then draft decks from it with friends. Communities in other TCGs have thriving platforms for it, but Riftbound doesn't yet have a dedicated home for the format. Cubebound aims to be that home — a place to construct cubes with full card search and filtering, analyze their balance across domains and costs, share lists publicly for others to browse and clone, and eventually draft them solo against bots or live with friends.

Cubebound is free to use, carries no ads or paywalled content, and exists to grow the Riftbound community and encourage organized play. Card data and imagery remain the property of Riot Games.
